# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-18

- Modifier-only global activation with Option + Shift and two alternate pairs.
- Live 8-bit sRGB HEX and RGB sampling through ScreenCaptureKit.
- Compact readout beneath the cursor, connected by a centered arrow with display-edge handling.
- Native Liquid Glass on macOS 26 and later; ultra-thin material fallback on earlier supported versions.
- Copy current or last HEX, local hotkey preference, and permission status in the menu.
- Reduce Motion support, serialized capture, and stale-result invalidation on release.
- Universal Intel/Apple silicon build and glass prism droplet app icon.
