# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-19

- Local color history with recency deduplication, favorites, names, deletion, and 100 recent colors plus protected favorites.
- Fourteen export formats with alpha, accurate sRGB/OKLab/ColorSync conversions, and a persistent default.
- Native image import, drop, and clipboard paste; asynchronous deterministic OKLab k-means palette extraction and editable saved palettes.
- WCAG 2.x contrast preview, alpha compositing over an explicit canvas, AA/AAA checks, swapping, and accessible foreground suggestions.
- Compact native Color Studio and independent feature switches in Settings and the menu bar.
- Clean history action menus with the overlapping native arrow removed.
- Automated conversion, persistence, PNG/JPEG/HEIC extraction, clipboard, integration, and original picker regression tests.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-18

- Modifier-only global activation with Option + Shift and two alternate pairs.
- Live 8-bit sRGB HEX and RGB sampling through ScreenCaptureKit.
- Compact readout beneath the cursor, connected by a centered arrow with display-edge handling.
- Native Liquid Glass on macOS 26 and later; ultra-thin material fallback on earlier supported versions.
- Copy current or last HEX, local hotkey preference, and permission status in the menu.
- Reduce Motion support, serialized capture, and stale-result invalidation on release.
- Universal Intel/Apple silicon build and glass prism droplet app icon.
